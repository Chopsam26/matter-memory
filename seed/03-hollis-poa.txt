DURABLE POWER OF ATTORNEY
MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS

I, MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS, of 14 Willow Lane, Concord, Middlesex County,
Massachusetts, appoint the agent named below to act for me in any lawful way
with respect to the powers granted in Article III of this instrument.

ARTICLE I - APPOINTMENT OF AGENT

Section 1.1. I appoint my daughter, CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT, of Lexington,
Massachusetts, as my Agent, to act as my attorney-in-fact.

Section 1.2. If Claire is unable, unwilling, or unavailable to serve, I appoint
my sister, RUTH HOLLIS ALDEN, of Providence, Rhode Island, as my successor
Agent, with the same authority.

Section 1.3. I have deliberately not appointed my son, DANIEL ROBERT HOLLIS, as
Agent or successor Agent, and he shall not serve in either capacity under any
circumstances.

Section 1.4. Only one Agent shall serve at a time. A successor Agent may
establish their authority to act by an affidavit stating that the prior Agent
is unable, unwilling or unavailable to serve.

ARTICLE II - EFFECTIVE DATE AND DURABILITY

Section 2.1. This power of attorney is effective immediately upon execution and
is not conditioned upon my incapacity.

Section 2.2. This power of attorney is DURABLE. It shall not be affected by my
subsequent disability or incapacity, in accordance with Massachusetts General
Laws Chapter 190B, Article V.

Section 2.3. This power of attorney terminates upon my death, upon my written
revocation delivered to the Agent, or upon the entry of a court order
terminating it.

ARTICLE III - POWERS GRANTED

I grant my Agent full power and authority to act on my behalf with respect to
the following, subject always to the limitations in Article IV:

Section 3.1. Banking. To open, maintain and close accounts in my name; to
deposit and withdraw funds; to endorse and negotiate checks and other
instruments; and to access any safe deposit box held in my name.

Section 3.2. Real property. To manage, lease, insure, repair and pay taxes on
any real property in which I hold an interest, and to sell or convey such
property, provided that any conveyance of my residence at 14 Willow Lane
requires the written concurrence of the successor Agent named in Section 1.2.

Section 3.3. Investments. To buy, sell, and hold securities; to exercise voting
rights; and to establish, direct and close brokerage accounts.

Section 3.4. Trust funding. To transfer any asset of mine into THE MARGARET A.
HOLLIS REVOCABLE TRUST dated March 14, 2022, and to execute any document
reasonably necessary to complete such a transfer.

Section 3.5. Taxes. To prepare, sign and file federal, state and local tax
returns; to receive confidential tax information; and to represent me before
any taxing authority.

Section 3.6. Government benefits. To apply for and receive Social Security,
Medicare, Medicaid and other government benefits on my behalf.

Section 3.7. Claims and litigation. To assert, defend, settle or compromise any
claim by or against me, and to retain counsel for that purpose.

ARTICLE IV - LIMITATIONS ON THE AGENT'S AUTHORITY

Section 4.1. My Agent may NOT make gifts of my property except gifts to my
descendants that do not exceed, in any calendar year, the federal gift tax
annual exclusion amount then in effect for any one recipient.

Section 4.2. My Agent may NOT make any gift, loan, or transfer of my property
to DANIEL ROBERT HOLLIS, in any amount and for any purpose.

Section 4.3. My Agent may NOT change the beneficiary designation on any life
insurance policy, retirement account, annuity, or account payable on death.

Section 4.4. My Agent may NOT create, amend, revoke or terminate THE MARGARET
A. HOLLIS REVOCABLE TRUST, notwithstanding the funding authority granted in
Section 3.4.

Section 4.5. My Agent may NOT exercise any power that would cause the Agent to
be treated as the owner of my assets for federal income or estate tax purposes.

Section 4.6. My Agent may NOT delegate their authority under this instrument to
any other person, other than by resignation in favor of the successor Agent
named in Section 1.2.

ARTICLE V - AGENT'S DUTIES AND COMPENSATION

Section 5.1. My Agent shall act in good faith, within the scope of the
authority granted, and in my best interest. My Agent shall keep my property
separate from the Agent's own property.

Section 5.2. My Agent shall keep a complete record of all receipts,
disbursements and transactions made on my behalf, and shall make that record
available to me, to any court of competent jurisdiction, and to the successor
Agent upon request.

Section 5.3. My Agent shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed
for reasonable expenses actually incurred in carrying out the authority granted
here.

ARTICLE VI - RELIANCE BY THIRD PARTIES

Section 6.1. Any third party may rely upon a copy of this instrument certified
by my Agent to be a true copy, to the same extent as upon the original.

Section 6.2. No third party who relies in good faith on the authority of my
Agent under this instrument shall incur any liability to me, my estate, or my
successors as a result of that reliance.

Section 6.3. I revoke all powers of attorney previously executed by me,
including the general power of attorney dated June 2, 2014.

Signed this 14th day of March, 2022, at Concord, Massachusetts.

                                        MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS

Witnessed and acknowledged before me on the date above.

                                        PRIYA N. RAGHAVAN, Notary Public
