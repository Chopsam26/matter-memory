SECTION 1 - GRANTOR AND GRANTEE

MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS, an unmarried person, of 14 Willow Lane, Concord,
Middlesex County, Massachusetts (the "Grantor"),

for consideration paid of One Dollar ($1.00) and other good and valuable
consideration, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged,

grants to MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS, as Trustee of THE MARGARET A. HOLLIS REVOCABLE
TRUST under Declaration of Trust dated March 14, 2022, of 14 Willow Lane,
Concord, Middlesex County, Massachusetts (the "Grantee"),

with QUITCLAIM COVENANTS, the land described in Section 2 below.

SECTION 2 - DESCRIPTION OF THE PREMISES

The land in Concord, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, with the buildings
thereon, known as and numbered 14 Willow Lane, being shown as Lot 7 on a plan
entitled "Plan of Land in Concord, Mass., owned by Alden R. Whitcomb," dated
September 12, 1948, recorded with the Middlesex South Registry of Deeds in Plan
Book 704, Plan 31, and bounded and described as follows:

  NORTHERLY  by Willow Lane, one hundred twelve and 40/100 (112.40) feet;

  EASTERLY   by Lot 8 on said plan, two hundred six and 15/100 (206.15) feet;

  SOUTHERLY  by land now or formerly of the Concord Conservation Commission,
             one hundred nine and 80/100 (109.80) feet;

  WESTERLY   by Lot 6 on said plan, two hundred eleven and 05/100 (211.05)
             feet.

Containing 22,940 square feet, more or less, according to said plan.

SECTION 3 - TITLE REFERENCE

For Grantor's title see the deed of ROBERT JAMES HOLLIS and MARGARET ANNE
HOLLIS, as tenants by the entirety, dated May 22, 1987, recorded with the
Middlesex South Registry of Deeds in Book 18402, Page 117.

Title vested in MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS individually by operation of law upon the
death of ROBERT JAMES HOLLIS on February 11, 2019. See Certificate of Death
recorded in Book 72905, Page 44.

SECTION 4 - ENCUMBRANCES

The premises are conveyed subject to:

  (a) Real estate taxes assessed by the Town of Concord for the current fiscal
      year, which are not yet due and payable.

  (b) An easement in favor of Concord Municipal Light Plant for utility lines
      along the northerly boundary, recorded in Book 21188, Page 502.

  (c) Restrictions of record set forth in Book 704, Plan 31, so far as the same
      are now in force and applicable.

The premises are conveyed free of any mortgage. The mortgage granted to
Merchant & Coastal Bank recorded in Book 41007, Page 219 was discharged of
record on November 30, 2016, by discharge recorded in Book 68331, Page 90.

SECTION 5 - PURPOSE OF CONVEYANCE

This conveyance is made for estate planning purposes only, without
consideration, and transfers the premises from the Grantor individually to the
Grantor in her capacity as Trustee. It is exempt from the Massachusetts deeds
excise tax under M.G.L. c. 64D, s. 1, as a transfer without consideration to a
revocable trust of which the Grantor is the settlor.

The Grantor retains full beneficial use and occupancy of the premises during
her lifetime under Section 3.1 of the Declaration of Trust.

SECTION 6 - TRUSTEE'S CERTIFICATE

The undersigned certifies that she is the sole Trustee of THE MARGARET A.
HOLLIS REVOCABLE TRUST dated March 14, 2022; that the trust has not been
revoked, terminated or amended; and that she has full power and authority to
acquire, hold, mortgage and convey the premises described above.
