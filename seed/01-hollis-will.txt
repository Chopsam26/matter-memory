LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS

I, MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS, of 14 Willow Lane, Concord, Middlesex County,
Massachusetts, being of sound mind and memory, do hereby make, publish and
declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, hereby revoking all wills and
codicils previously made by me.

ARTICLE I - IDENTIFICATION OF FAMILY

I was married to ROBERT JAMES HOLLIS, who died on the 11th day of February,
2019. I have not remarried.

I have two children now living: my daughter, CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT, of
Lexington, Massachusetts, and my son, DANIEL ROBERT HOLLIS, of Portland,
Oregon. I have one grandchild now living, OWEN BENNETT, the minor son of my
daughter Claire, born September 3, 2016.

References in this will to my "descendants" shall be construed in accordance
with Article V, which sets out my intention with respect to my son Daniel.

ARTICLE II - PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay from my residuary estate all of my
legally enforceable debts, the expenses of my last illness and funeral, and the
costs of administering my estate, as soon after my death as is practicable.

I further direct that all estate, inheritance, transfer and succession taxes
payable by reason of my death shall be paid out of my residuary estate as an
expense of administration, without apportionment and without any right of
reimbursement from any recipient of property.

ARTICLE III - SPECIFIC BEQUESTS

Section 3.1. I give my diamond engagement ring, my pearl necklace, and all
other articles of personal jewelry to my daughter CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT, if she
survives me.

Section 3.2. I give my Steinway model M grand piano, serial number 412886, to
my grandson OWEN BENNETT. If Owen is a minor at the time of my death, I direct
that the piano be held by his parent or guardian until he attains the age of
twenty-one years.

Section 3.3. I give the sum of Fifteen Thousand Dollars ($15,000) to the
Concord Free Public Library Corporation, to be used for the general purposes of
the library.

Section 3.4. I give all of my remaining tangible personal property, including
household furnishings, books, and automobiles, to my daughter CLAIRE HOLLIS
BENNETT, if she survives me. Any such property she disclaims shall pass under
Article IV.

ARTICLE IV - RESIDUARY ESTATE

All the rest, residue and remainder of my property, of every kind and
description and wherever situated, including any property over which I hold a
power of appointment, I give to the then-serving Trustee of THE MARGARET A.
HOLLIS REVOCABLE TRUST, established by me under a Declaration of Trust dated
March 14, 2022, as that trust may be amended from time to time, to be added to
the principal of that trust and administered and distributed in accordance with
its terms.

This is a pour-over provision. It is my intention that my residuary estate be
governed by the terms of the trust described above and not distributed under
this will. If for any reason that trust is not in existence at my death, or is
held invalid, then I give my residuary estate to my daughter CLAIRE HOLLIS
BENNETT, and if she does not survive me, to her descendants then living, by
right of representation.

ARTICLE V - INTENTIONAL OMISSION

I have intentionally made no provision under this will for my son, DANIEL
ROBERT HOLLIS, or for any of his descendants.

This omission is intentional and is not the result of accident, mistake, or any
failure to recollect the existence of my son. I have considered the matter
carefully and with the advice of counsel. My reasons are set out in a separate
written statement of even date delivered to my attorney and are not repeated
here.

Any person who contests this will, or who assists any other person in doing so,
shall forfeit any interest given to them under this will or under the trust
described in Article IV, and the forfeited interest shall pass as though that
person had predeceased me leaving no descendants.

ARTICLE VI - FIDUCIARY APPOINTMENTS

Section 6.1. I nominate my daughter CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT to serve as Personal
Representative of my estate.

Section 6.2. If Claire is unable or unwilling to serve, or ceases to serve, I
nominate ELEANOR M. VANCE, ESQ., of Vance & Reid LLP, Concord, Massachusetts,
to serve as successor Personal Representative.

Section 6.3. I direct that no Personal Representative serving under this will
be required to furnish surety on any bond.

ARTICLE VII - GUARDIAN

If at my death any minor descendant of mine has no living parent, I nominate
CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT as guardian of the person and property of that minor. If
Claire is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate RUTH HOLLIS ALDEN, my
sister, of Providence, Rhode Island.

ARTICLE VIII - EXECUTION

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have signed this will, consisting of five typewritten
pages including this page, on the 14th day of March, 2022, at Concord,
Massachusetts.

                                        MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS

The foregoing instrument was signed by the Testatrix in our presence, and we,
at her request and in her presence and in the presence of each other, have
subscribed our names as witnesses on the date last above written. We each
declare that the Testatrix appeared to us to be of sound mind and under no
constraint or undue influence.

        HANNAH P. OKONKWO, of Concord, Massachusetts
        DAVID L. MERRITT, of Acton, Massachusetts
