DECLARATION OF TRUST
THE MARGARET A. HOLLIS REVOCABLE TRUST

This Declaration of Trust is made on the 14th day of March, 2022, by MARGARET
ANNE HOLLIS, of Concord, Massachusetts, as Settlor and as initial Trustee.

SECTION 1 - CREATION AND NAME

Section 1.1. The Settlor hereby declares that she holds and will hold the
property described in Schedule A, together with any property later added, in
trust under the terms of this Declaration.

Section 1.2. This trust shall be known as THE MARGARET A. HOLLIS REVOCABLE
TRUST dated March 14, 2022.

Section 1.3. This trust is revocable. The Settlor reserves the right to amend
or revoke it in whole or in part at any time during her lifetime by a signed
writing delivered to the then-serving Trustee.

SECTION 2 - TRUST PROPERTY

Section 2.1. The initial trust property consists of the real property located
at 14 Willow Lane, Concord, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, conveyed to the
Trustee by quitclaim deed dated April 6, 2022 and recorded with the Middlesex
South Registry of Deeds at Book 79214, Page 388.

Section 2.2. The Settlor may add property to this trust at any time. Property
passing to the Trustee under Article IV of the Settlor's will shall be added to
the principal of this trust and administered under Section 4.

Section 2.3. The Trustee shall maintain the property at 14 Willow Lane in good
repair and shall pay all real estate taxes, insurance premiums and reasonable
maintenance costs from trust income, and from principal to the extent income is
insufficient.

SECTION 3 - DISTRIBUTIONS DURING THE SETTLOR'S LIFETIME

Section 3.1. During the Settlor's lifetime, the Trustee shall distribute to the
Settlor, or apply for her benefit, as much of the net income and principal as
the Settlor directs.

Section 3.2. If the Settlor becomes incapacitated, the Trustee shall distribute
so much of the income and principal as the Trustee determines to be necessary
or advisable for the Settlor's health, education, maintenance and support,
taking into account her accustomed standard of living and all other resources
known to the Trustee.

Section 3.3. For purposes of this Declaration, the Settlor shall be considered
incapacitated upon the written certification of two licensed physicians, at
least one of whom is board certified in neurology, geriatrics, or psychiatry,
that she is unable to manage her financial affairs.

SECTION 4 - DISTRIBUTION UPON THE SETTLOR'S DEATH

Section 4.1. Upon the death of the Settlor, the Trustee shall first pay from
the trust estate any expenses of the Settlor's last illness and funeral, and
any death taxes payable by reason of the Settlor's death, to the extent the
probate estate is insufficient to pay them.

Section 4.2. The Trustee shall then distribute the real property at 14 Willow
Lane, Concord, Massachusetts, together with all insurance and escrow accounts
relating to it, outright and free of trust to the Settlor's daughter, CLAIRE
HOLLIS BENNETT, if she survives the Settlor by thirty days.

Section 4.3. The Trustee shall divide the remaining trust estate into two
shares:

  (a) Seventy percent (70%) shall be distributed outright to CLAIRE HOLLIS
      BENNETT, if she survives the Settlor by thirty days.

  (b) Thirty percent (30%) shall be retained in trust for the Settlor's
      grandson, OWEN BENNETT, and administered under Section 5.

Section 4.4. The Settlor has made no provision under this trust for her son,
DANIEL ROBERT HOLLIS, or for his descendants. This omission is intentional and
is consistent with Article V of the Settlor's will of even date. The no-contest
provision in Article V of that will applies equally to any interest arising
under this trust.

SECTION 5 - THE OWEN BENNETT SHARE

Section 5.1. The Trustee shall hold the share set aside under Section 4.3(b)
and may distribute income and principal for Owen's health, education,
maintenance and support.

Section 5.2. The Trustee shall distribute one-half of the then-remaining
principal to Owen when he attains the age of twenty-five years, and the balance
when he attains the age of thirty years.

Section 5.3. If Owen dies before complete distribution, the remaining balance
shall be distributed to his then-living descendants by right of
representation, and if none, to CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT, and if she is not then
living, to the Concord Free Public Library Corporation.

SECTION 6 - TRUSTEES

Section 6.1. MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS shall serve as initial Trustee.

Section 6.2. Upon the Settlor's death, resignation, or incapacity as determined
under Section 3.3, CLAIRE HOLLIS BENNETT shall serve as successor Trustee.

Section 6.3. If Claire is unable or unwilling to serve, RUTH HOLLIS ALDEN,
the Settlor's sister, shall serve as successor Trustee. If neither is able to
serve, the then-serving Personal Representative of the Settlor's estate shall
appoint a corporate fiduciary authorized to do business in Massachusetts.

Section 6.4. No Trustee serving under this Declaration shall be required to
furnish bond or surety in any jurisdiction.

SECTION 7 - SPENDTHRIFT PROVISION

No beneficiary of any trust created under this Declaration may assign,
anticipate, pledge or otherwise transfer any interest in income or principal
before it is actually distributed. No such interest shall be subject to the
claims of any creditor of a beneficiary, or to legal process, prior to actual
receipt by the beneficiary.

SECTION 8 - GOVERNING LAW

This Declaration and the trusts created under it shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

Executed as a sealed instrument on the date first written above.

                                        MARGARET ANNE HOLLIS
                                        Settlor and Initial Trustee

SCHEDULE A - INITIAL TRUST PROPERTY

  1. Real property at 14 Willow Lane, Concord, Massachusetts, as described in
     Section 2.1.
  2. The sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00) in hand.
