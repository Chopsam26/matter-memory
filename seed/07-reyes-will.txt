LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF THOMAS ANDRES REYES

I, THOMAS ANDRES REYES, of 88 Prospect Hill Avenue, Somerville, Middlesex
County, Massachusetts, declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, and
revoke all wills and codicils previously made by me.

ARTICLE I - IDENTIFICATION OF FAMILY

I am married to ELENA MARISOL REYES, and all references in this will to "my
wife" are to her.

I have two children now living: my son, MATEO ANDRES REYES, of Somerville,
Massachusetts, and my daughter, SOFIA REYES OYELARAN, of Brooklyn, New York. I
have no deceased children and no other descendants.

ARTICLE II - PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

I direct my Personal Representative to pay from my residuary estate my legally
enforceable debts, the expenses of my last illness and funeral, and the costs
of administering my estate.

All estate and inheritance taxes payable by reason of my death shall be paid
from my residuary estate without apportionment.

ARTICLE III - SPECIFIC BEQUESTS

Section 3.1. I give my collection of woodworking tools, including the Oliver
pattern maker's lathe housed in the garage workshop, to my son MATEO ANDRES
REYES, if he survives me.

Section 3.2. I give my grandfather's guitar, a 1961 Ramirez classical
instrument, to my daughter SOFIA REYES OYELARAN, if she survives me.

Section 3.3. I give the sum of Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000) to the Somerville
Community Boating Fund.

Section 3.4. I give all my remaining tangible personal property to my wife
ELENA MARISOL REYES, if she survives me, and if she does not, in as nearly
equal shares as practicable to my children who survive me.

ARTICLE IV - RESIDUARY ESTATE

Section 4.1. If my wife ELENA MARISOL REYES survives me by sixty days, I give
my entire residuary estate to her outright.

Section 4.2. If my wife does not survive me by sixty days, I give my residuary
estate in equal shares to my children, MATEO ANDRES REYES and SOFIA REYES
OYELARAN, who survive me, and to the descendants by right of representation of
any child of mine who does not survive me.

Section 4.3. If any share under Section 4.2 would pass to a beneficiary who has
not attained the age of twenty-three years, that share shall instead be held by
my Personal Representative under a custodianship pursuant to the Massachusetts
Uniform Transfers to Minors Act until that age.

Section 4.4. I have made no provision in this will for any person not named in
Article I. This will makes no gift to any charity except as stated in Section
3.3.

ARTICLE V - REAL PROPERTY

Section 5.1. My wife and I hold the property at 88 Prospect Hill Avenue,
Somerville, Massachusetts, as tenants by the entirety. That property passes to
my wife by operation of law and is not disposed of by this will.

Section 5.2. If my wife does not survive me, so that the property at 88
Prospect Hill Avenue forms part of my estate, I direct that it be sold and the
proceeds added to my residuary estate, unless both of my children agree in
writing within six months of my death that one of them shall take it, in which
case that child shall take it subject to an obligation to equalize the other
child's share out of the residue.

ARTICLE VI - FIDUCIARY APPOINTMENTS

Section 6.1. I nominate my wife ELENA MARISOL REYES as Personal Representative
of my estate.

Section 6.2. If my wife is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate my son
MATEO ANDRES REYES as successor Personal Representative.

Section 6.3. If neither is able to serve, I nominate ELEANOR M. VANCE, ESQ., of
Vance & Reid LLP, Concord, Massachusetts.

Section 6.4. No Personal Representative serving under this will shall be
required to furnish surety on any bond.

ARTICLE VII - GUARDIAN

If at my death I leave a minor child, and my wife does not survive me, I
nominate my sister-in-law CAMILA OYELARAN, of Brooklyn, New York, as guardian
of the person and property of that child.

ARTICLE VIII - EXECUTION

IN WITNESS WHEREOF I have signed this will, consisting of four typewritten
pages including this page, on the 22nd day of September, 2023, at Somerville,
Massachusetts.

                                        THOMAS ANDRES REYES

The foregoing instrument was signed by the Testator in our presence, and we, at
his request and in his presence and in the presence of each other, have
subscribed our names as witnesses on the date last above written.

        GRACE T. LINDQVIST, of Somerville, Massachusetts
        OMAR B. HADDAD, of Medford, Massachusetts
